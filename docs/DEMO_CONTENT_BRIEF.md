# Two-minute demo content brief

This is an editorial proposal, not a new render. Text overlays only; actual 3D plant visualizer, no decorative kickers or footers. Use the research report as the source for claims.

## 0–30 seconds: explain the job and experiment

0–10: Show a recognizable 3D plant and its feed/products. Caption: “Can AI agents design a more valuable chemical separation plant?”

10–20: Highlight an actual column change and simulator feedback. Caption: “Propose a design. Simulate it. Inspect the result. Improve it.” Label value as simulated annualized net value.

20–30: Introduce single agent and swarm side by side. Caption: “We tested what agents share—and how they share it.”

## 30–65 seconds: show an actual evolution

Show a recorded single-agent and swarm example using the actual 3D plants. Prefer the newly available V3 dev002 plant records. Keep the camera fixed, highlight changed equipment, and update each saved best-so-far value. Label the task and each run's own progress; do not imply equal elapsed time or equal team queries unless the source timestamps or counters support that alignment. The V2 replay remains an alternative clearly labeled as an earlier example.

## 65–100 seconds: lead with the final V3 dev002 results

Show the actual best plants with a compact, task-specific leaderboard: Kimi 50-agent islands 3.866 at 2,496 solves; DeepSeek 50-agent islands 2.160 at 2,349; Opus solo 5.331 at 74. Include Kimi solo 1.754 at 196 as the same-model reference. Caption: “Our best team designs improved with more search. A strong solo agent remained ahead.”

Show Kimi's 13-column-position winner, then contrast retained design diversity: 16 best structures across the 50-agent run versus one for ten-agent open sharing. Caption: “Quality and diversity depend on how search is organized.” These are observed outcomes; historical migration defects mean periodic exchange is not a proven cause. Keep the resource counts visible.

## 100–120 seconds: research takeaway

Briefly connect the earlier dev000 memory controls: the shared swarm had a higher endpoint with more queries, solo led at equal queries, and extra Past retrieval did not improve endpoints. Finish on the final plant designs. Caption: “What should agents share—and how do we preserve exploration?”
